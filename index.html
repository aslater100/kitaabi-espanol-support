Kitaabi Español

Kitaabi Español is a study app for Spanish, with daily listening, speaking, reading, vocabulary and sentence practice. It runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac. On the App Store it's called "Kitaabi: Spanish Learning".

Support

To report a bug or suggest a feature, open Settings → Feedback & bug reports in the app. You can also email kitaabi.ios@gmail.com.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Kitaabi Español works offline and has no sign-up. If you use iCloud, your progress syncs between your devices.

What does the Claude API key do?

The key is optional. With your own Anthropic key, the app uses Claude to write new practice passages and example sentences. Anthropic bills your account for this use. The rest of the app works without a key.

Is this the same app as Kitaabi?

No. Kitaabi is the Arabic app, with its own support page. Kitaabi Español is a separate app for Spanish, from the same developer.
